  The K columns are what a disturbance quotient would ask for and are reported as a
  diagnostic, not a requirement. A saturated joint reports a lower bound on its torque
  rather than a measurement of it, and at an unsaturated joint the logged torque is
  dominated by the policy's own commanded offset rather than by anything external, so
  neither case yields a disturbance. Section 11.1 gives the reconstruction that
  separates the two and the reason this table decides no gain in this document.
